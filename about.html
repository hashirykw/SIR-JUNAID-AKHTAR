About

History as evidence. Geography as a map.

Sir Junaid Akhtar teaches Cambridge Pakistan Studies at O Level and IGCSE. He describes himself as Cambridge-trained in the UK, and he is the Director of SWK Solutions in Karachi.

The method

Paper 1 is not a memory test. Cambridge gives you a source, or a claim to weigh, and rewards you for explaining. Sir Junaid teaches 2059 that way: what a source says, what it leaves out, who wrote it and what they wanted from the reader. It is a technique, and it can be learned.

Paper 2 works the same way with maps, graphs and photographs. The marks go to students who read the resource in front of them and tie it to what they know: why the canal is lined, why the city grew, what the dam costs downstream.

The notes

His Section 1 and Section 3 notes for Pakistan Studies are passed between students across Pakistan, often a long way from his own classrooms. His students get the current versions, matched to the past-paper questions they answer.

The work with Cambridge

He trained with Cambridge in the UK and has told his students he worked alongside its exam team there, which is why his advice tends to start with the mark scheme.
